CivicSignal
Agenda watch for your town

Your city decided the parking rules on your street last Tuesday. Was anyone watching?

Council and board agendas go up days before the vote - dense PDFs, posted once, easy to miss. CivicSignal reads the agendas for your city, county, or school board and alerts you the day your keywords appear: your street, your business category, your issue. Before the vote, not after.

How it works

1. Name your place + keywords"Maple Street," "short-term rental," "food truck," "zoning" - the words that decide whether a meeting matters to you.
2. We read every agendaCity council, planning commission, county board - each new agenda and minutes packet, checked as it posts.
3. You get the signalWhat's being considered, when the vote is, which item number, and the link to the full packet.
